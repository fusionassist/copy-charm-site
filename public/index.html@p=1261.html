FAQ

What types of digital displays do you offer

We provide a wide range of digital display solutions, including:

  • Interactive touchscreens
  • High-brightness window displays
  • Video walls
  • Free-standing kiosks
  • Meeting room solutions
    Each product is designed to meet specific business needs across various industries
How do I create and manage content for my display?

You can design and schedule content using our user-friendly content management system (CMS). This platform allows you to:

  • Upload media files
  • Create playlists
  • Schedule content playback
  • Monitor display status
    For detailed guidance, refer to our user manuals or contact our support team.
Can I schedule content to play at specific times?

Absolutely. Our CMS offers advanced scheduling features, enabling you to:

  • Set start and end times for content
  • Define recurrence patterns (daily, weekly, etc.)
  • Apply time windows for content display
    This flexibility ensures your audience sees the right message at the right time
