## What we mean by "digital signage"

Any screen that delivers content to an audience in a public or commercial space — wayfinding in
a hospital, the menu over a QSR counter, the product reel in a shop window, the schedule
overhead in a station, the welcome at a corporate reception.

We supply, install and support the hardware. The screens — and the cabling, mounts, enclosures,
players and networks that make them work — are what we do, all day.
