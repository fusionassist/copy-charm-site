## About the role

You'll be on-site with our installation team across the 32 counties, fitting digital signage,
interactive touchscreens, LED video walls and self-ordering kiosks for our clients — from a
single screen in a Letterkenny coffee shop to a 40-panel wall in a Limerick stadium.

This is a hands-on, customer-facing role. You'll arrive at a site, scope the install with the
client, run cabling, mount hardware, commission and test, and leave the customer trained on the
content system. Some weeks you'll be home every night; some weeks you'll be on a multi-site
rollout three counties over.

## What we're looking for

- **2+ years** in AV, telecoms, retail fit-out or electrical install work
- Comfortable with wall mounts, cable management, basic carpentry, occasional working at height
- Clean B / B+E driving licence (we'll provide a van)
- A "leave it neater than you found it" mentality — IDI's reputation is built on installs that
  feel finished
- Comfortable explaining things to non-technical staff

## Nice to have

- Network basics (VLANs, PoE, basic switch config)
- Experience with content management systems for digital signage
- Trade qualification (electrical, carpentry) — not required, useful

## What you get

- €38k–€55k depending on experience
- Company van + fuel card + mobile + laptop
- 25 days' leave + bank holidays
- Pension contribution after probation
- Training budget for industry certifications (CTS, network certs)
- A team that gets stuck in and supports each other

## How to apply

Send a CV and a few sentences about your favourite install you've worked on to
`sales@interactivedisplays.ie` with **"AV Installation Engineer"** in the subject line.
