## Sunlight-readable signage for the shopfront window

A screen in a window fights the sun — and an ordinary screen loses. Moytronix Ultra High
Brightness Window Displays are built for exactly this job: **3,000 cd/m² panels** — around ten
times brighter than a domestic TV — that keep your offers vivid and legible even with direct
sunlight on the glass.

Just as important is what the sun *doesn't* do to them. Standard LCDs placed behind glass suffer
the blackening defect as panel temperatures climb; these displays use **TNI commercial panels
that withstand surface temperatures up to 110°C**, with internal temperature management, so they
run all day in the window, all year, 24/7.

### Five sizes, one job

From a **43″** unit for a pharmacy window to a **75″** statement screen for a car showroom, every
size shares the same DNA: Full-HD panel, 178° viewing angles, portrait or landscape mounting, an
ambient light sensor, and a slim enclosure with VESA mounting. A hanging ceiling-mount system and
a double-sided hanging version are available for displays suspended in the window itself.

### Built-in Android, managed by ScreenFusion

There's no media player to hide behind the screen — **Android 11** is built in, on the
Rockchip RK3568 quad-core platform used across the whole Moytronix screen range. Load content by USB
plug-and-play, or go with the network version and run everything through **ScreenFusion**, IDI's
cloud signage platform: schedule campaigns, swap offers in seconds, and monitor every window
screen in every branch from a browser. Network versions include a Wi-Fi signal booster in the box, and an upgraded connectivity
board — Wi-Fi 6 dual-band, Gigabit Ethernet, Bluetooth 5.4 — or an **Android 14** platform
upgrade are available on request.

### Supplied, installed and supported by IDI

You've probably walked past one of these already — pharmacies, estate agents, county council
libraries and leisure centres, salons, cafés and takeaways across Ireland run their windows on
this range (that's them in the gallery above).

Window installations are precision work — glare angles, safe suspension, power routing, and a
screen that faces the public 24/7. Our own nationwide install team handles all of it, and every
display carries our standard **3-year parts-and-labour warranty** with lifetime technical support.
