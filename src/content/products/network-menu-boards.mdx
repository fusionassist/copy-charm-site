## Commercial digital menu screens — in stock, installed nationwide

**Digital menu screens** put your menu on bright, commercial-grade displays instead of printed or
backlit boards — so you can change prices in seconds, daypart breakfast to lunch automatically, and
show allergens clearly. We supply them on our own **Moytronix** commercial panels and install them
nationwide with our own engineer team.

They're running **Android with ScreenFusion**, IDI's cloud signage platform, so the media player is
built into the screen — no external box, no clutter — and you manage every screen from a browser.

> **In stock now.** We're holding stock of both 43-inch and 32-inch menu screens, so we can survey,
> supply and install within days — not weeks. Ideal for a launch, refit or busy season.

### Built for foodservice

- **43-inch and 32-inch** commercial panels — mix sizes across a counter run
- **500-nit anti-glare** screens, readable under bright deli and forecourt lighting
- **24/7 rated** for all-day, every-day operation — with a **3-year warranty**
- **Landscape or portrait** mounting, ultra-slim and close to the wall
- **ScreenFusion** cloud CMS: schedule dayparts, update prices, roll out to every site at once
