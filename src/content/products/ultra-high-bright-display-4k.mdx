## The shopfront that stops traffic — in 4K

When the window screen is two metres wide, resolution stops being a spec-sheet detail and
becomes the difference between "impressive" and "pixelated". The Moytronix 4K Ultra High
Brightness Window Display puts a **3840 × 2160 panel at 3,000 cd/m²** behind your glass —
sunlight-readable, razor-sharp at nose-to-the-window distance, and running 24/7.

It shares its DNA with our standard TNI window range: a **TNI commercial panel** that resists
the blackening defect in direct sun, internal temperature management, and **Android 11** built
in on the Rockchip RK3568 platform — no external media player, managed remotely through
**ScreenFusion**, IDI's cloud signage platform.

### Built for the big frontage

Car showrooms, flagship retail, banks, auctioneers' windows, hotel façades — anywhere the
window itself is the advertising space. Landscape or portrait, wall-mounted or suspended, and
paired on request with our hanging window-mount systems.

**Need 4K in a different size?** 4K panel options are available across the window display
range on request — tell us the size and we'll quote it.

### Supplied, installed and supported by IDI

Every display carries our **3-year parts-and-labour warranty** with lifetime technical
support, delivered and installed nationwide by IDI's own engineer team.
