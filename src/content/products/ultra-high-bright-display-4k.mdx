## The window range, in 4K

The Moytronix 4K Ultra High Brightness Window Display puts a **3840 × 2160 panel at
3,000 cd/m²** behind your glass — sunlight-readable, razor-sharp at nose-to-the-window
distance, and running 24/7.

It shares its DNA with our standard TNI window range: a **TNI commercial panel** that resists
the blackening defect in direct sun, internal temperature management, and **Android 11** built
in on the Rockchip RK3568 platform — no external media player, managed remotely through
**ScreenFusion**, IDI's cloud signage platform.

### Made to order, in every size

The 4K versions are supplied to order across the full window range — **43″, 49″, 55″, 65″ and
75″**, landscape or portrait, wall-mounted or suspended with our hanging window-mount systems.
Tell us the size and we'll quote it.

Not sure you need 4K? For most single-screen shopfronts our
[standard Full-HD TNI range](/product/ultra-high-bright-display) is the right call — same
brightness, same panel protection, off-the-shelf.

### Supplied, installed and supported by IDI

Every display carries our **3-year parts-and-labour warranty** with lifetime technical
support, delivered and installed nationwide by IDI's own engineer team.
