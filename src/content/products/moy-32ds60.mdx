## Compact commercial signage, player built in

The **Moy-32DS60** brings the same all-in-one Moytronix signage platform to a compact 32-inch panel.
A genuine BrightSign player is built into the display, so there's no external media box, no extra
power supply and no extra cabling — ideal for menu boards, wayfinding, reception screens and any
spot where a larger display simply won't fit.

It's part of the Moytronix **Moy-DS60 Series**, manufactured on the TCL MOKA commercial platform
and supplied, installed and supported in Ireland by Interactive Displays Ireland.

### Small footprint, full commercial spec

- **BrightSign HS145 built in.** Industry-standard signage playback and BrightSignOS reliability,
  with self-healing software and free updates — no separate player to buy or fail.
- **500-nit anti-glare panel.** Stays readable in bright interiors and rated for 24/7 duty, just
  like its larger sibling.
- **2K commercial panel.** A crisp Full-HD-class picture with 4000:1 dynamic contrast and a 178°
  viewing angle. (For a true 4K screen, step up to the 43-inch [Moy-43DS60](/product/moy-43ds60).)
- **Light and slim.** At 5.4 kg with a 40 mm symmetrical body and 8.8 mm bezel, it mounts easily in
  portrait or landscape.

### Manage it from anywhere

Every Moy-DS60 ships with a free BSN.cloud Control Cloud subscription, so you can push content and
monitor every screen remotely — and it works with your choice of BrightSign-compatible CMS.
Dual-antenna 2.4/5 GHz Wi-Fi and Ethernet keep displays reliably online; HDMI, USB and RS-232 are
on board too.

### Supplied, installed and supported by IDI

The Moy-32DS60 comes with our standard **3-year parts-and-labour warranty**, and our nationwide
install team handles wall mounting, network setup and CMS training — the same team many other Irish
signage suppliers rely on for their own installs.
