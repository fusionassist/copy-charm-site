## The Irish-assembled GAA scoreboard

**CluScore** is our LED scoreboard system built specifically for GAA clubs and community sports
grounds — as installed at Moylagh GAA (shown above). It shows the score the way GAA actually keeps
it — **goals and points** — alongside a match clock and team names on a bright, outdoor LED display,
with a dedicated panel for club sponsors.

It's designed and assembled in Ireland, and supplied, installed and supported by Interactive
Displays Ireland.

### Built for match day

- **Proper GAA scoring** — goals and points, match clock, and team names, clear from across the pitch
- **Controlled from a phone or laptop** — a volunteer updates the score on the sideline, no specialist kit
- **Welcome screen & sponsor adverts** — greet supporters before throw-in and rotate sponsor graphics or video
- **A revenue earner for the club** — the built-in sponsor panel helps fund the board and beyond
- **More than GAA** — supports soccer, rugby and other codes for multi-sport grounds
- **Outdoor-rated LED on a steel frame** — bright in daylight, built for Irish weather, installed nationwide

### Supplied, installed and supported by IDI

Every CluScore board is installed by our own nationwide team and backed by our 3-year warranty. We
handle the display, the steel frame, the control app and the sponsor setup — the club just plays.
