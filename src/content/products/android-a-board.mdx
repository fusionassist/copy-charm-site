## Built for shopfronts, events and forecourts

The Android A-Board is purpose-built for places where a wired display isn't an option. Two
swappable batteries, a 2,500-nit LCD that stays readable under direct sun, and an IP65 enclosure
that handles Irish weather without blinking.

Content updates over Wi-Fi or 4G — schedule promotions in advance, push last-minute changes from
a phone, run scheduled playlists.

### Where it works best

- **Retail forecourts** — promote offers without a long-throw projector or scaffolded sign
- **Outdoor events** — wayfinding, sponsor reels, schedules
- **Hospitality entrances** — daily menus, group bookings, "today's special"
- **Pop-up trade stands** — your brand wall, but updateable

### What you get

Every Android A-Board ships with our 3-year warranty, in-house support, and optional install via
the IDI nationwide team.
