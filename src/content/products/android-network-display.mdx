## Professional Android signage, running ScreenFusion

Moytronix professional displays put commercial-grade content on the wall with the player built
straight into the screen — no external media box, no extra cabling, no clutter. Each display runs
**Android** with **ScreenFusion**, IDI's own cloud signage platform, so you can schedule playlists,
push changes and monitor every screen from any web browser.

They're built on a commercial panel manufactured for us by TCL MOKA, and supplied, installed and
supported in Ireland by Interactive Displays Ireland.

### Two sizes, one platform

- **Moy-43DS60 — 43″, true 4K UHD.** 3840 × 2160 detail for menus, product shots and brand video
  that stop people mid-stride.
- **Moy-32DS60 — 32″, 2K.** The same platform in a compact panel for menu boards, wayfinding,
  reception and room signage where a larger screen won't fit.

Both share the same commercial DNA: a **500-nit anti-glare panel** rated for round-the-clock 24/7
duty, a **4000:1 dynamic contrast ratio** and a **178° viewing angle**, in an ultra-slim 40 mm
symmetrical chassis with a narrow bezel that sits almost flush to the wall.

### Managed by ScreenFusion

ScreenFusion is IDI's cloud content-management platform. Build and schedule content, group screens
by site or zone, and monitor status remotely — all from a browser, with no on-site PC or separate
player to maintain. Dual-antenna 2.4/5 GHz Wi-Fi and Gigabit-class Ethernet keep every display
reliably online; HDMI, USB and RS-232 are on board for local sources and integration.

### Supplied, installed and supported by IDI

Every display comes with our standard **3-year parts-and-labour warranty**, and our nationwide
install team handles wall mounting, network setup and ScreenFusion training. Many other Irish
signage suppliers outsource their installations to that same team.
