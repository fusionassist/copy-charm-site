## Commercial 4K signage with the player built in

The **Moy-43DS60** is a professional 43-inch digital signage display that puts true 4K Ultra-HD
content on the wall — with a genuine BrightSign player built directly into the screen. There's no
external media box to mount, power or hide, and no extra cabling to run. Just a slim, symmetrical
anti-glare panel that mounts in minutes and runs the content you already have.

It's part of the Moytronix **Moy-DS60 Series**, manufactured on the TCL MOKA commercial platform
and supplied, installed and supported in Ireland by Interactive Displays Ireland.

### Why it's built for real signage — not a TV on a wall

- **BrightSign HS145 built in.** Industry-standard signage playback and BrightSignOS reliability,
  with self-healing software and free updates — no separate player to buy or fail.
- **500-nit anti-glare panel.** Stays readable in bright retail and hospitality spaces, rated for
  round-the-clock 24/7 duty.
- **True 4K clarity.** 3840 × 2160 resolution with a 4000:1 dynamic contrast ratio and 178° viewing
  angle, so menus, product shots and brand video look sharp from every angle.
- **Ultra-slim, ultra-narrow bezel.** A 40 mm symmetrical body and 8.7 mm bezel sit almost flush to
  the wall for a clean, content-first look.

### Manage it from anywhere

Every Moy-DS60 ships with a free BSN.cloud Control Cloud subscription, so you can push content and
monitor every screen remotely — and it works with your choice of BrightSign-compatible CMS.
Dual-antenna 2.4/5 GHz Wi-Fi and Gigabit-class Ethernet keep displays reliably online; HDMI, USB
and RS-232 are on board for local sources and integration.

### Supplied, installed and supported by IDI

The Moy-43DS60 comes with our standard **3-year parts-and-labour warranty** — and our nationwide
install team handles wall mounting, network setup and CMS training. Many other Irish signage
suppliers outsource their installations to that same team.
