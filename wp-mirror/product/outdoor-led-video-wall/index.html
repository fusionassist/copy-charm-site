Outdoor LED Video Wall

Make a bold visual statement with the Outdoor LED Video Wall, engineered for maximum visibility and performance in any weather condition. Featuring an exceptional 6,000cd/m² brightness and seamless SMD-LED technology, your content remains vivid and eye-catching even under direct sunlight.

Built with an IP-rated weatherproof enclosure, this video wall stands up to rain, dust, and extreme temperatures, ensuring year-round durability and uninterrupted performance. Its advanced video processing delivers brilliant, distortion-free imagery with enhanced colour fidelity.

The slim and lightweight modular panels are designed for easy installation and maintenance, with both front and rear access options. With remote cloud-based CMS control, you can manage, schedule, and monitor your content across locations—anytime, anywhere.

For added efficiency, the optional ambient light sensor automatically adjusts brightness based on outdoor lighting conditions, helping you save energy without compromising impact.
For rental options and managed touchscreen solutions, visit rentascreen.ie and screenfusion.ai.

Applications for Outdoor LED Video Wall

  • Retail Facades & Shopfronts – Attract foot traffic with dynamic promotional content
  • Stadiums & Sports Arenas – Broadcast scores, sponsors, and live action
  • City Centres & Landmarks – Eye-catching public information and advertising
  • Transport Hubs (Airports, Train Stations) – Digital signage for crowds and commuters
  • Outdoor Events & Concerts – Immersive stage backdrops and audience engagement
  • Corporate Campuses & Buildings – Brand messaging and wayfinding on a grand scale
